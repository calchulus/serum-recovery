Serum v3 Open-Orders Recovery

Withdraw unsettled balances and stranded rent left behind in Serum v3 open-orders accounts. Runs entirely in your browser — your private key never leaves your wallet and this page never sees it.

Read before use. This signs real mainnet transactions. It builds exactly two Serum instructions — settleFunds and closeOpenOrders — and lists every account in them before you sign. Check that list in your wallet’s preview too. Each transaction is simulated before you are prompted, and broadcast with preflight enabled so a failing transaction is rejected rather than landed.

1 · RPC endpoint

Stored only in this browser’s localStorage; never sent anywhere except the endpoint itself. The scan uses getProgramAccounts, which free public endpoints refuse (403 Indexed requests require a personal token). Use Helius, Triton, QuickNode, or your own node.

2 · Wallet

Inspect mode analyses an address without connecting, so you can audit any wallet. Signing stays disabled unless the connected wallet owns the account.

The vault signer is create_program_address([market, u64_le(nonce)]) using the nonce stored in the market, not a canonical bump PDA.
